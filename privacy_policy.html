2.3 Photos, Face Data and AI Processing. PhotoMgi-Ai provides AI-powered photo generation and editing features. When you choose to upload, select, or provide a photo in the Application, the photo may include a person's face. We process such photos only to provide the specific AI feature requested by you, such as generating, editing, enhancing, or transforming an image.

The face-related data we may process is limited to the facial image information contained in the photo you choose to provide. We do not use face data to identify you or any other person, verify identity, authenticate users, track users, or create a biometric profile. We do not collect, create, store, or share faceprints, face geometry templates, facial recognition identifiers, or other biometric identifiers.

Before a photo is processed for an AI feature, the Application asks for your permission where required or requires you to actively select or upload the photo. You may choose not to provide a photo, but some AI photo features may not be available without it.

Uploaded photos and any face-related data contained in them are processed through the Application and secure servers operated by us, as needed to provide the requested AI feature. This information is retained only for the time necessary to provide the requested AI feature, maintain service security, troubleshoot technical issues, or comply with legal obligations. Unless a longer period is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or user support, such data is deleted or de-identified after the AI processing is completed.

Generated images may be stored on your device or in your account if the Application provides a save, history, or cloud-sync feature. You may delete generated content from the Application where deletion features are available, or contact us at photomgi.ai@gmail.com to request deletion of personal data associated with your use of the Application.
