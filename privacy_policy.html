Piclume Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Piclume and DAOWEI NETWORK TECHNOLOGY PTE. LTD. (collectively, “we,” “us,” or “our”) collect, use, and protect information when you use the Piclume application and related services. You may change applicable permissions or consent choices in your device or app settings.

1. Information you provide

We may receive information you provide directly, such as account details, support requests, feedback, and other communications. If account functionality is available, we may process the information needed to create and maintain that account.

2. Device and usage information

We may collect limited technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, language, network information, crash records, diagnostics, and interactions with features. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Piclume.

3. Photos, face data, and AI processing

Piclume processes photos you select only to provide the editing, enhancing, replacement, style, background, object removal, or face-related feature that you request. Face-related data is limited to the image content needed to perform the selected feature; we do not use it to identify you or build a facial-recognition profile.

Selected images may be sent securely to service providers that help us perform AI processing. They may process that content only as needed to provide the requested result and under applicable contractual safeguards. Results may be stored on your device or in your account where that option is available.

4. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve Piclume features.
  • Respond to support questions and communicate service information.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, security incidents, and technical problems.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with vendors that support hosting, analytics, customer service, security, payment processing, or AI photo processing. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a corporate transaction.

6. Retention and security

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required by law. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal information. You can also manage photo access and other permissions through your device settings. To submit a request, contact us using the address below.

8. Children

Piclume is not directed to children where parental consent would be required by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

9. International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version with a new “Last updated” date and provide additional notice when required by law.

11. Contact us

For questions or privacy requests, email photomgi.ai@gmail.com.
